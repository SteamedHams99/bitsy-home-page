About the book

Love, in the age of artificial intelligence

Dating has always been a leap of faith. But today, the person on the other side of the screen might be shaped by filters, prompts and suggestions — or might not be a person at all. The Idea of You explores what happens to romance when technology promises to give us exactly what we think we want.

Drawing on a lifetime of watching people fall in and out of love — and a front-row seat to how her own family and friends now meet, match and message — Cindy Meister asks a simple, unsettling question: when we can design the perfect partner, are we still falling for a person, or just for the idea of one?

  • Genre: Contemporary · Relationships · Technology
  • Format: Paperback & eBook
  • Published: 2026

Modern romance

Swipes, matches, read receipts and second chances — a clear-eyed look at how people search for love today.

Artificial intelligence

What happens when the algorithm knows your type better than you do — and starts writing your messages for you?

Real connection

Beneath the technology, a story about the very human need to be seen, known and chosen.

Meet the author

Hi, I’m Cindy.

Former attorney, proud mom of two daughters, grandmother, and a lifelong Chicagoland local.

After years of reading contracts, I finally got to write the story I’d been thinking about for a long time. The Idea of You grew out of watching the people I love try to find each other in a world full of apps, algorithms and AI — and wondering what all of it means for the heart.
